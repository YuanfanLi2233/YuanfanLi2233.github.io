Research

Representation Learning

Contrastive learning turns paired observations into low-dimensional embeddings, yet its success depends on both the structure of the data distribution and the downstream task. I study this pipeline end to end: what the population objective represents, how well a finite-dimensional model can approximate it, whether the learned score is calibrated for retrieval, and how finite samples and negative sampling affect generalization.

  • PMI & spectral structure
  • low-dimensional embeddings
  • retrieval consistency
  • negative sampling

Deep Neural Networks

I investigate how optimization dynamics translate into statistical guarantees for deep networks. My published work analyzes gradient descent for deep ReLU classification and obtains near-optimal sample-size rates with polynomial depth dependence. Recently, I have been focusing on quantized neural networks, studying how quantization affects the optimization and generalization of neural networks.

  • gradient descent
  • deep ReLU networks
  • optimal rates
  • quantization
  • straight-through estimators

LLM alignment

Preference optimization is usually analyzed under a correctly specified probabilistic model, even though real human preferences may be cyclic or otherwise incompatible with that assumption. I study the statistical consequences of this mismatch and how they interact with model approximation and finite data. More broadly, I am interested in the mathematical mechanisms underlying the success of reasoning models.

  • DPO
  • preference misspecification
  • alignment
  • post-training
