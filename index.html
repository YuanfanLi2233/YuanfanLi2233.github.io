Statistical Learning Theory & Optimization

Yuanfan Li 李远凡

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at The University of Sydney, advised by Professors Yiming Ying and Ding-Xuan Zhou.

I develop theory for how modern learning systems represent data, generalize from finite samples, and behave under optimization. My current interests span representation learning, deep and quantized neural networks, and the alignment and post-training of large language models.

Research at a glance

Full research overview →

Representation Learning

Approximation, consistency, and generalization of contrastive learning, from PMI and spectral structure to downstream retrieval.

Deep & Quantized Networks

Optimization and generalization of deep ReLU networks, with ongoing work on straight-through estimators and quantized training.

LLM Alignment & Post-training

Statistical limits of preference optimization and theoretical questions in model alignment and post-training.

Featured preprint

Statistical Learning Theory for Direct Preference Optimization

Joel Wills, Yuanfan Li, Yiming Ying, and Ding-Xuan Zhou.

We ask what DPO can learn when human preferences do not follow the Bradley-Terry model. A triangle-curl diagnostic isolates irreducible structural misspecification from neural-network approximation and finite-sample estimation.
