Yuanfan Li 李远凡

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at The University of Sydney, advised by Professors Yiming Ying and Ding-Xuan Zhou.

My research focuses on statistical learning theory and optimization for modern machine learning. I am particularly interested in understanding the approximation, generalization, and training dynamics of deep learning models, with applications to representation learning, quantized neural networks, and the alignment of large language models.
